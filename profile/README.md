# SMU‑Quantum 🦁

**Empowering the future of quantum computing—one qubit at a time.**

Welcome to the official research repository for Quantum Computing at **Singapore Management University (SMU)**. Directed by [Prof. Hoong Chuin Lau](https://faculty.smu.edu.sg/profile/lau-hoong-chuin-631), our lab sits at the intersection of quantum theory and industrial application, specializing in **Quantum Optimization** and **Hybrid Classical-Quantum Algorithms**.

## 🤝 Collaboration & Partnership

Achieving "Quantum Advantage" requires a multidisciplinary ecosystem. We actively partner with academic institutions and industry leaders to push the boundaries of what is computationally possible.

### 🔬 Research Synergy
* **Academic Collaboration:** We co-author high-impact publications and develop novel algorithms in quantum optimization and machine learning.
* **Industry Pilots:** We apply our proprietary solvers to complex challenges in **Supply Chain, Finance, and Logistics**, moving quantum theory into production.
